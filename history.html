The story so far

League History

Five seasons of expansion, rivalries and unforgettable championship moments.

2022

The beginning

SWL launches with four teams: Biscuits, Wolverines, Sluggers and Brew Crew.

2023

Back-to-back

The Pigeons enter the league, and the Sluggers win a second straight title.

2024

Six-team expansion

The Pitchers and Hustlers join. The Hustlers capture their first championship.

2025

Eight teams

The Outlaws and Underdawgz arrive. The Pitchers win their first title.

2026

Historic comeback

Hammerheads and Curve Ballers debut. The Sluggers complete a reverse sweep in the final.

The reverse sweep

After losing the first two games 10–7 and 9–8, the Sluggers stormed back with wins of 8–6, 10–2 and 12–5 to capture their third championship. Ian Schrock was named Championship MVP.

A new champion

The Pitchers defeated the Biscuits in the championship series to win their first SWL title. Clay Nohel earned both Season MVP and Championship MVP honors.

🏆 CHAMPION

Bowling Green Biscuits

Expansion team to champion

The Hustlers swept the Pitchers in the semifinals before sweeping the Biscuits in the championship. Roger Pharris earned Championship MVP; Tanner Kremer was Season MVP.

🏆 CHAMPION

Southcentral Sluggers

RUNNER-UP

Bowling Green Biscuits

Back-to-back titles

The Sluggers defeated the Brew Crew and then swept the Biscuits in the best-of-five championship to defend their title. Alex Bison was both Season MVP and Championship MVP.

The inaugural champions

In the SWL’s first season, the Sluggers swept the Biscuits to claim the league’s inaugural championship. Alex Bison received both MVP awards.
