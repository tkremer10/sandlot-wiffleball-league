Championship History

From four founding teams to a six-team league, the SWL has built its own traditions, rivalries, and postseason legends.

2022

Where it started

Four original clubs: Biscuits, Wolverines, Sluggers, and Brew Crew. Champion: Sluggers
MVP: Alex Bison.

2023

Sluggers repeat

Pigeons joined in place of the Wolverines. Sluggers swept Brew Crew, then Biscuits. Champion: Sluggers
MVP: Alex Bison.

2024

Expansion season

Pitchers and Hustlers entered the league; four of six teams made the playoffs. Champion: Hustlers
MVP: Tanner Kremer.

2025

A new era

Outlaws and Underdawgz expanded the league to eight teams. Champion: Pitchers
MVP: Clay Nohel.

2026

The comeback

Hammerheads and Curve Ballers joined a refreshed lineup; Sluggers returned under new management. Champion: Sluggers
MVP: Chris Schrock.
